# Frameworks, Tools, and Strategies for Implementing Decentralized TinyMLOps Architectures

## Context

The aim of this repository is to complement our systematic literature review study focusing on *Frameworks, Tools, and Strategies for Implementing Decentralized TinyMLOps Architectures*. A dedicated journal article details relevant information including research design, findings and discussion. *Currently, the contributed article is under review.* However, all data supporting the findings of our study and the tool for automatic creation of systematic maps and graphs presented in the study can be publicly accessed in this repository.
